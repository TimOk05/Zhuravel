06 Before you book

A few useful
answers.

Which areas do you cover?

Zhuravel is a South Coast mobile valeting service. Send your postcode on Instagram to confirm availability for your exact location.

How is the price worked out?

The quote can reflect the vehicle type, its current condition and the level of care you need. Sharing a few photos helps make the recommendation more accurate.

What should I send with my enquiry?

Include your vehicle type, postcode area, the main areas you want addressed and, where useful, a few current photos of the car.

What is needed on the day?

Access and setup can vary by location. Any practical requirements will be confirmed with you before the appointment is agreed.
